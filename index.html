Edit
Nickname & notification email
Nickname and email can be updated. Bank details cannot be edited — delete and re-add to prevent fraudulent redirects.
Delete
Requires confirmation
Requires a confirmation tap. Active or scheduled transfers are not cancelled. Deleted contacts cannot be restored.
Why can't I edit bank details?
Routing/account changes require re-entry to prevent fraudulent redirects. Delete the contact and add a new one with updated details.

ACH vs wire — when to use which
Use ACH for regular payments where no fee and 1–3 day settlement is fine.

Use Wire for time-sensitive or large-value transfers where same-day delivery justifies the $25 fee.
